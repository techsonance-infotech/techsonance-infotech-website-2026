# Project Ideas & Blueprints

## Typical MVP Estimates & Scopes
- **Mobile MVP (iOS & Android with Flutter)**: Cross-platform mobile app with authentication, user profiles, notifications, offline sync, and payment gateway. Typically 6 to 10 weeks.
- **SaaS Platform MVP**: Multi-tenant web platform with authentication, dashboard analytics, role management, and automated subscription billing. Typically 8 to 12 weeks.
- **Custom AI / RAG Chatbot**: Document indexing, vector search, custom system guardrails, natural language query answering, and meeting booking integration. Typically 2 to 4 weeks.
- **B2B E-Commerce Marketplace**: Multi-vendor catalogs, checkout, payment processing, seller dashboard, and shipping integrations. Typically 8 to 12 weeks.
- **Custom POS & Inventory System**: Offline-first point-of-sale, barcode scanning, live multi-terminal sync, and automated GST billing. Typically 6 to 10 weeks.

## How We Scope New Ideas
If you have a rough idea or wireframes, we help distill it into a phased roadmap: Phase 1 (Core MVP to validate with users), Phase 2 (Advanced features and automation), and Phase 3 (Scaling infrastructure).
