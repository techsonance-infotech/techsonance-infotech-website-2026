# Pricing & Engagement Models

## Engagement Models
We offer three flexible and transparent engagement structures:
1. **Fixed-Price Milestone Contracts**: Best for projects with clear requirements. Defined scopes, guaranteed delivery dates, and payments tied to approved deliverables.
2. **Monthly Dedicated Teams / Developers**: Best for evolving products and fast iterations. Full-time dedicated engineers (Next.js, Flutter, Node.js, AI) focused exclusively on your backlog.
3. **Time & Material (T&M)**: Best for technical consulting, architecture audits, or rapid prototyping.

## Factors Influencing Project Cost
Costs vary based on platform scope (Web, iOS, Android), feature complexity, third-party integrations (payments, maps, SMS), AI/LLM integration depth, and timeline urgency.

## Free Initial Consultation & Proposal
We offer a 100% free 30-minute discovery consultation. We review your idea and deliver a transparent scope breakdown, architecture suggestion, and cost estimate with zero obligation.

## Payment Terms & Methods
Payments are divided across project milestones (e.g. deposit at kickoff, sprint milestones upon approved demo, and final balance at release). We accept Bank Wire Transfers (NEFT, RTGS, SWIFT), UPI, Wise, PayPal, and Stripe.
