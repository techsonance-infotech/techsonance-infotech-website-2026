# Team & Engineering Structure

## Engineering Team & Roles
We are a focused, senior-led software development team. Our core roles include full-stack web engineers (React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript), mobile app developers (Flutter for iOS & Android), UI/UX product designers, QA automation testers, and DevOps cloud engineers. Our founder remains directly involved in technical architecture and quality reviews for every client engagement.

## Seniority & Experience
Our lead engineers possess over 5+ years of production experience building high-scale architectures, real-time messaging, distributed caching, multi-tenant databases, and AI LLM integrations.

## In-House Delivery & Dedicated Points of Contact
All primary engineering, architecture, design, and DevOps are executed directly in-house without outsourcing core tasks. Every client project is assigned a dedicated technical project manager or engineering lead who provides transparent communication, bi-weekly demos, and sprint progress tracking.

## Staff Augmentation & Dedicated Developers
Clients can hire dedicated full-time or part-time developers (Next.js, Node.js, Flutter) working exclusively on their products under their own sprint cadence and timezone preferences.
